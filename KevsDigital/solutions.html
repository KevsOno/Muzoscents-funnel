Solutions

One platform, applied to the realities of your industry.

Kevs bOS is designed for mid-market retail, pharmacy and food-service operators. The platform is the same; the way it is configured, used and prioritised differs by industry.

Supermarkets & grocery

High-SKU operations where margin is decided by accuracy.

Supermarkets and grocery retailers carry thousands of SKUs across perishable and non-perishable categories. Small operational errors — an expiry missed, a count that drifts, a reorder placed too late — accumulate into significant margin loss over a month.

  • Batch-level inventory with expiry monitoring
  • FEFO rotation recommendations for shelf staff
  • POS and offline sales continuity
  • Procurement and reorder visibility
  • Branch-level reporting for multi-store operators

Operational outcomes

Fewer unnoticed expiry losses. Cleaner stock counts. Better-informed purchasing. Branch performance visible to management.

Operational record-keeping

Kevs bOS provides expiry alerts, FEFO recommendations and timestamped records that support operational and compliance evidence. It does not replace a pharmacy's regulatory obligations or legal advice.

Pharmacies

Batch-level expiry intelligence and a clear operational record.

Pharmacies face a different operational profile from supermarkets: regulated products, tighter batch-level tracking, and a documented expectation of accountability. Kevs bOS is built around batch-level inventory, expiry visibility and a timestamped record of operational actions.

  • Batch-level expiry intelligence
  • FEFO rotation recommendations
  • Timestamped action and disposition records
  • Inventory management with branch visibility
  • Role-based operational accountability

bOS supports the operational process of identifying, acting on and documenting at-risk stock. It is not a substitute for professional compliance or regulatory advice.

Restaurants & food service

Order flow, inventory and fulfilment, coordinated.

Restaurants and food-service operators manage a fast-moving operational environment: orders, kitchen coordination, stock depletion, delivery and customer experience all happening at once. Kevs bOS brings these into a single operational layer.

  • Sales and order processing
  • Inventory consumption and stock visibility
  • Procurement and supplier management
  • Fulfilment and operational coordination

Restaurant-specific capabilities described on this page are limited to those supported by the product documentation. Additional food-service features should be discussed directly with our team.

One operational layer

Orders, stock, and fulfilment visible together — so daily operational decisions are made from a single source of information.

Organisation-level control

Branch scoping and organisation-level visibility, built into the platform's access model rather than added as a reporting workaround.

Multi-branch retail

One view across every branch. Clear boundaries between them.

Multi-branch businesses need both central visibility and local autonomy. Kevs bOS supports an organisation structure with distinct branches, each with its own staff, stock and reporting, connected to central management oversight.

  • Organisation-level reporting across branches
  • Branch-scoped access for local staff
  • Location-specific inventory and stock transfers
  • Connected reporting and exception visibility
