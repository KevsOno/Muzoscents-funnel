Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Kevs Digital ("we," "our," or "us") provides enterprise software and business systems, including our flagship product, Kevs bOS. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you visit our website, interact with us commercially, or use the Kevs bOS platform.

We are committed to handling personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and applicable data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

Website Visitors: When you browse this website or submit a demonstration request, we may collect your name, business email address, phone number, business name, industry, and operational details you voluntarily provide.

Platform Users (Client Data): When your organisation uses Kevs bOS, we process business operational data (inventory, sales, procurement) and the personal data of your staff required to provision access (names, emails, roles). In this context, your organisation is the Data Controller, and Kevs Digital acts as the Data Processor.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange product demonstrations.
  • To provision and secure client accounts on the Kevs bOS platform.
  • To provide technical support and maintain platform integrity.
  • To improve our software, services, and website experience.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating the platform (e.g., cloud hosting providers, email delivery services) under strict data processing agreements. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

Kevs bOS is built with a security-conscious architecture, including database-level tenant isolation, organisation- and branch-scoped access control, and hardened web security headers. Physical data security and local access control remain the responsibility of the client organisation.

6. Your Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below. If you are a staff member of a client organisation, please contact your employer first, as they control your data on the platform.

7. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Notice, please contact:
Kevs Digital
Email: privacy@kevsdigital.com

Note: This Privacy Notice is a draft template prepared for the Kevs Digital website. It should be reviewed and finalised by legal counsel to ensure full compliance with applicable regulations (such as NDPA 2023) and to include specific company details (e.g., physical address, registration number).
