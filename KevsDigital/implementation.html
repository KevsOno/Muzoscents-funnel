Implementation

Technology alone does not transform operations.

Kevs bOS is delivered through a co-managed implementation model, a structured process that recognises the responsibilities on both sides of a successful deployment.

The co-managed model

What each side brings to the deployment.

A co-managed implementation means the client owns the human and physical side of operations, and Kevs Digital owns the digital platform. Neither side can succeed without the other.

Kevs Digital

  • Platform configuration
  • Data onboarding and catalog preparation
  • Digital infrastructure and synchronisation integrity
  • Technical support for the platform
  • Training delivery
  • System audit trails

Client

  • Local hardware and network
  • Accurate business data
  • A named operations owner
  • Staff participation in training
  • Physical inventory discipline
  • Enforcement of operational processes
The Buying Process

From first conversation to full deployment.

We don't do hard sells or overnight installs. Our commercial process is designed to ensure Kevs bOS is the right fit for your operations before you make a commitment.

1

A short conversation to understand your business, current systems, and key challenges. We assess whether your volume, catalogue size, and IT baseline match our deployment requirements.

2

A live walkthrough of Kevs bOS tailored to your industry. We focus on the specific modules and workflows that address the problems you raised during discovery.

5

After deployment, you transition to a monthly subscription. This covers platform access, updates, and technical support, with regular reviews to ensure you are getting maximum value.

The five-stage framework

A deployment method built around adoption.

Software fails in operations for one main reason: adoption. The five-stage model addresses that directly, beginning with why the system matters and ending with reinforcement against the client's own baseline.

1

Awareness

The owner, the named operations owner and key staff understand why the system is being deployed, using the client's own operational context and numbers, not generic product messaging.

2

Desire

Staff see what they stop doing, manual reconciliation, repeated stock checks, informal order tracking. The system's value is framed around the work it removes, not the features it adds.

3

Knowledge

Hands-on training using the client's own data. The FEFO rotation process and location discipline policy are walked through in person, not left to documentation.

4

Ability

A two-week parallel operational run. The previous process continues in parallel while the team builds familiarity. Exceptions are reviewed daily and operational gaps are resolved before full cutover.

5

Reinforcement

Day-30 and day-60 reviews against the client's operational baseline. Discipline is enforced by management, supported by the system's audit trail, not delegated to the software alone.

Parallel run

Two weeks. Both processes live. Exceptions reviewed daily.

The parallel run is not an optional phase. It is a controlled window during which the client's previous operational process and Kevs bOS run side by side. Differences, inventory exceptions, order discrepancies, reporting gaps, are reviewed daily rather than discovered at month-end.

  • Both processes operating in parallel
  • Daily exception review during the window
  • Controlled cutover once exceptions are understood

Technical support

Two clearly defined support responsibilities.

Kevs Digital supports the platform. The client's in-house IT supports local hardware and network. Keeping those responsibilities distinct is what allows each to be handled properly.

Client-side (Level 1)

Local hardware, printers, POS terminals, network connectivity and physical equipment. Handled by the client's in-house IT, the person closest to the problem and best placed to resolve it quickly.

Platform-side (Level 2 & 3)

Platform issues, synchronisation integrity, data anomalies, API issues and platform security. Handled by Kevs Digital, the team responsible for the underlying system.
