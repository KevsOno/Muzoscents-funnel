How the partnership works

You open doors. We handle the technical delivery.

Kevs bOS is delivered as a co-managed business operating system for mid-tier operators in Nigeria. Partners act as commercial Introducers who identify qualified businesses, introduce decision-makers and stay close to the relationship.

Kevs Digital and our certified implementation team handle catalog preparation, system setup, staff training and ongoing platform support. You do not configure software, troubleshoot hardware or make unapproved technical promises.

Clear Role Boundaries

Partner (Introducer): Warm introductions, qualification routing and relationship continuity.

Kevs Digital (Implementer): Data onboarding, workflow setup, training and platform operations.

Commission Structure

Tiered recurring revenue with no time cap while active.

Partners earn on paying branch subscriptions and collected setup fees, subject to programme terms and active partner status.

18% to 30% Recurring Share

Monthly commission scales by active paying branch count: 18% for 1 to 10 active clients, 22% for 11 to 20, 25% for 21 to 30, and 30% for 31 or more active clients.

No Time Cap While Active

Earn monthly recurring commission for as long as your introduced client remains an active paying subscriber and you remain an active partner under programme terms.

20% Setup Commission

Earn a flat 20% commission on setup fees actually collected, paid within 3 business days after client payment clears into our account.

Who We Are Looking For

Built for connectors with real relationships in Nigeria.

We are rolling out our partner programme in Nigeria with a focus on retail, pharmacy and food service businesses.

Strong Fit

Independent B2B sales consultants, agency leads, distributor or FMCG connectors and retail advisers based in Nigeria (or able to meet owners in person) who know supermarket, pharmacy, grocery or restaurant decision-makers and can introduce real prospects.

Important Expectations

This is an independent, commission-only partnership, not a salaried job, with no fixed salary or guaranteed income. Full commission rules, lead protection and partner obligations are shared during onboarding and governed by the Partner Agreement.

Commission-Only Notice: There is no salary, retainer or guaranteed income in this role. Shortlisted applicants are invited to a brief call where full commission terms and expectations are reviewed.
